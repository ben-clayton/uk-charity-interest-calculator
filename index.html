Charity Legacy Interest Calculator

Calculates interest owed on a legacy to a charity where payment was delayed beyond the first anniversary of the date of death. Uses simple interest on the original amount, at the Court Funds Office (CFO) Basic Account rate. All calculation happens in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

Source code & how it works

Day count: 365 days/year, simple interest on the original amount — matching the original spreadsheet. Whether the CFO Basic Account rate is the legally correct rate for a given legacy claim should be confirmed with the charity's legal advisors.
